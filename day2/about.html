About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture ideas and reminders before you forget them. It helps you keep short notes organized and easy to review.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes.
  2. Type your idea or reminder in the note box.
  3. Click "Add Note" to save your note.

Features

  • Create quick notes
  • Keep reminders in one place
  • Use keyboard shortcuts
  • Send feedback about QuickNotes

Keyboard shortcuts
